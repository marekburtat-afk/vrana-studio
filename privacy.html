Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Vrana Studio is a private tool operated by a single creator for his own channel Edgar Vrána. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

Only data of the creator's own accounts that he connects himself: the basic channel or profile information needed to publish (account ID and name) and the permission to upload, schedule and publish his own videos and posts. Vrana Studio does not access, collect or process data of any other person, including viewers or followers.

How the data is used

Solely to upload and schedule the creator's own content at the times he chooses. Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Google user data

Vrana Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Vrana Studio uses the YouTube API Services and by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the creator disconnects the account; then they are deleted from the computer. To ask a question or request deletion, contact vrana.edgar.yt@gmail.com.
