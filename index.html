Vrana Studio

Vrana Studio is a private desktop tool used by one creator to plan and publish content of his own channel Edgar Vrána (an animated educational channel in Czech) on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

What it does

The creator places finished videos, reels, carousels and stories into a calendar and approves them. Vrana Studio then uploads and schedules each piece on the creator's own accounts through the official platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API, Facebook Pages API, TikTok Content Posting API).

Who can use it

Nobody else. The app is not distributed, not offered for download and does not let other people sign in. It runs only on the creator's own computer and only connects the creator's own accounts.
